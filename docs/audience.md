# Target audience (ICP)

Who we write for. Every post, keyword, and CTA should serve one of these people. This drives tone (specific, opinionated, no fluff) and which clusters we build first.

## Primary personas

1. **Indie founders / solopreneurs** shipping their own site or SaaS.
   - Pain: no SEO budget, no time, drowning in generic "ultimate guide" advice.
   - What they want: a free tool that tells them exactly what to fix, and plain-English guides.
   - Hook: "free, no signup, no crawl cap" + copy-paste fixes.

2. **Developers** who own a site/docs/portfolio and treat SEO as a config problem.
   - Pain: care about correctness (status codes, JSON-LD validity, canonicals, CWV) not marketing fluff.
   - What they want: technical accuracy, schema, robots/sitemap/llms.txt specifics.
   - Hook: the auditor's per-check explainers + the embeddable score badge.

3. **GEO-curious marketers / small-agency SEOs** waking up to AI search.
   - Pain: clients asking "why aren't we in ChatGPT/AI Overviews?"; SEO and GEO now share <20% of winners.
   - What they want: concrete GEO/AEO tactics that actually earn AI citations.
   - Hook: we are the GEO-first auditor — we audit for the exact things GEO requires.

## Why GEO-first

GEO/AEO is a newer, lower-competition space than broad SEO. A focused site can realistically reach #1 for GEO terms and earn AI citations within 4–8 weeks (Perplexity fastest). Broad "SEO audit/checker" head terms are owned by Ahrefs/Semrush/Moz and are a multi-year, backlink-heavy fight — so broad SEO content is the **funnel**, GEO is the **spearhead**.

## Voice

- Specific and tested over comprehensive. Numbers, named tools, real examples.
- Honest about limits (e.g., "ChatGPT can't crawl your live site").
- Always funnel to the free audit at `/` — it's the product and the proof.
- No keyword stuffing, no AI-filler tells, no "in today's fast-paced digital world."
