## Product Hunt — do this properly or not at all

Give it its own day. A half-hearted PH launch is worse than none.

1. Create the account **at least a week early** and comment on other launches. Brand-new accounts get filtered.
2. Prepare: a gallery image, a 30-second GIF of a live audit streaming in, the tagline above.
3. Launch **Tuesday–Thursday, 00:01 PT** — you get the full day's ranking window.
4. Post a maker comment immediately explaining *why* you built it.
5. Reply to every comment that day.

PH links are nofollow, so the direct SEO value is nil. The value is secondary coverage — newsletters and roundup posts that pick up PH launches **do** link, and those are the links that matter.

## What to skip

Paid "submit to 100 directories" services, link exchanges, and anything asking for a reciprocal link. Low-quality link networks are a real risk and they don't produce crawl demand anyway.
